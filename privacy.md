# Places Privacy Policy


Last updated: 16 November 2026

## The short version

- Places has no account or server operated by the developer to collect your timeline. Your timeline isn't automatically sent to the developer.
- Your timeline is stored on your iPhone, inside the app, protected by iOS encryption.
- There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data.

## What Places keeps on your iPhone

- The locations iOS gives Places while tracking is on, and the stops and journeys worked out from them.
- What you import: Google Maps Timeline exports, Arc Timeline and GPX files, notes files.
- Your notes, place names and labels, corrections, and the questions you ask Ask Places with their answers.
- Motion readings (still, walking, driving and so on) and step counts from Motion & Fitness, so older days can be rebuilt. iOS itself keeps these for only 7 days. Step counts are kept in a separate file that stays on this iPhone.
- Workouts and their routes, if you connect Apple Health. Places also reads the day's step count from Health to show it, without saving it.
- CarPlay and Bluetooth audio connections: the device's name (such as your car's) and when it was connected, which helps tell drives from other journeys. Places keeps a scrambled form of the device's identifier, never its address.
- For the "Where's my car" widget, while it's on your Home Screen: small map pictures of where your latest drive ended and where you are.
- A tracking log for Tracking health: when Places woke, battery level, and gaps in recording.
- Reports iOS gives Places about crashes, hangs, battery and background use (MetricKit), kept for 30 days for the diagnostics report.
- Original import files, recent searches, cached views and widgets, and date-only rules that keep automatically synced data from returning after deletion.

## When something leaves your iPhone

Places uses Apple services under Apple's privacy terms. Backups, exports and diagnostic sharing have their own destinations and controls.

- **Apple Maps** receives coordinates to draw the map and the "Where's my car" widget's map, name places, show place cards, Look Around and search results, and, with "Match drives to roads" on, to find the road between two location checks.
- **Apple Weather and Private Cloud Compute** are disabled in this version. Places doesn't make weather or cloud-model requests in this configuration. Apple Intelligence answers Ask Places on your iPhone.
- **Siri and Spotlight**: visited places, cities and countries are added to the system's Spotlight index on this iPhone, including imported places you haven't confirmed or labelled. Indexed facts include recorded first/last visits, visit counts and durations. Siri actions read the same local history as Ask Places. iOS controls Siri and search features.
- **iCloud Backup**: your timeline can be included in your iPhone's iCloud Backup when enabled. Step counts, backups saved inside Places and Ask Places answers are left out of it. You can exclude Places using Apple's per-app backup controls in iPhone Settings; Places cannot read or change that system setting.
- **Backups and exports**: file backups default to manual creation, with optional daily or weekly backups when Places is opened and a backup is due. Compressed files are saved to one location you choose in Storage & data. Backups kept inside Places stay on this iPhone, out of device backup. No backup or export contains Apple Health workouts, step counts or motion readings; Health provides the workouts again after a restore. Backups saved to a chosen folder also leave out car connections. Anyone with an export can read it; shared copies stay where you send them. Films, trip books (PDF), story cards, Year in review pages and route files you share carry the places, routes, maps and photos shown in them.
- **Diagnostics you send**: Tracking health › Send diagnostics and About Places › Contact support show a report you can read in full first: app and device details, settings, data counts, the last 14 days of the tracking log and what iOS reported about crashes and battery. It has times, counts and settings, never places, coordinates, addresses or notes. It goes only where you share or email it, and you can leave it out of a support email.
- **Crash reports**: if you choose to share iPhone Analytics with app developers, Apple may send the developer diagnostic and performance reports under those settings.

## Apple Health

- Places reads only workouts, workout routes and step counts, to show them in Activities, on your timeline and in the day summary. It saves workouts to Health only if you turn on saving recorded activities.
- Places doesn't use Health data for advertising or marketing and doesn't sell it. Imported workouts are stored in the local timeline database, which normal device backup can include, as described above. Backups and exports never include them, and step counts read from Health aren't saved.
- You can stop access at any time in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Places.
- Deleting a period in Places doesn't delete the original workouts from Apple Health. Date-only deletion rules prevent automatic sync from bringing that period back into Places, including after reconnecting Health. An explicit backup restore can replace these rules and recover history.

## Photos

- Places reads your photo library only after you turn on a photo setting in Settings › Photos or tap "Build from your photos". iOS asks for access the first time. Photos you pick for a trip film come through the system's photo picker, which doesn't open your library to Places.
- With "Show photos" on, Places shows your photos and videos on the timeline and map. They load from your library each time they're shown. This browsing does not copy, upload or change them.
- When you add a photo or music file to a trip film, Places saves an optimized photo copy or the selected audio file with that video project on this iPhone. When you start a new film and Places can already see your library, it fills the film for you: it picks the trip's photos taken along the route, judging them on this iPhone, and saves the same kind of copies, without their locations. One Undo removes them. These copies are included in your Places backups and data exports. Erasing the source trip's dates or all Places data removes the project and its copies. Removing an item from a film keeps its copy available for Undo; this does not change the original in Photos or Files.
- **Save Video to Photos** asks for add-only library access and adds the finished film when you choose it. Save to Files and Share use the destination you select. Rendered copies saved outside Places remain there until you remove them.
- With "Fill in routes from photos" on, Places saves when and where each photo was taken (not the photo itself) on this iPhone. Those places fill gaps between location checks and in stretches with nothing recorded. They never change your stops.
- When you delete a photo, or remove its location in Photos, Places forgets it and takes that point out of your routes. You can also tell Places not to use one photo's location. Turning the setting off makes Places forget every photo location, and you choose whether your routes keep the points.
- The photo locations that fill routes stay on this iPhone, in the timeline database. They're left out of exports, but your iPhone's own backup includes them along with the rest of your timeline. They go when you delete that period or erase your data.
- **Build from your photos** reads when and where each photo in your library was taken, on this iPhone, and adds stops for times nothing else on your timeline covers. Places saves only those stops and their places, not the photos or a list of where they were taken. Photos with a place and time you couldn't have been at, such as ones saved from a chat, are left out. The stops are one import, "Your photos", which you can undo in Settings › Imported data. Like other stops, they're part of your backups and exports.

## Your choices

- Location, Motion & Fitness, Photos and Health access can be changed at any time in the iOS Settings app. Places keeps working with less: you can still import history and add visits by hand.
- Photos, road matching, automatic backups and minute location checks can be turned off in Places settings. Weather and Private Cloud Compute aren't offered in this version.
- Notifications are made on this iPhone and arrive quietly, without a prompt: one if recording stops, and a "Welcome home" note after a trip, which names the place you came back from. Turn them off in iOS Settings › Notifications › Places.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- Local history is kept until you delete it. Backups and exported copies have separate lifetimes.
- Me › Settings › Storage & data › "Delete a date range" removes whole overlapping visits, trips and notes, location points in the range, overlapping step counts, relevant tracking logs and unused automatic place names. Tracking pauses briefly and resumes with your settings preserved; an overlapping recording continues as a new segment. Assistant answers, recent searches, original import files and backups remain and can contain information about the deleted period; remove these separately if needed.
- "Erase all timeline data" also removes saved places, assistant answers, recent searches, app-managed import/export files, local widget snapshots (including the "Where's my car" maps), where Places thinks home is for the "Welcome home" note, and Spotlight entries. It turns tracking and Health sync off and discards an active recording. You can keep backups for recovery or request removal of the app's backups, including its backups in the currently selected folder. Deletion failures are reported.
- Places retains only date ranges for automatic-import exclusions, with no coordinates or workout identifiers. These rules stop reconnects or rebuilding from silently restoring deleted periods. An explicit restore replaces these rules with those saved in the backup and can recover deleted history.
- Deleting the app deletes everything stored in it. Copies you saved elsewhere stay until you delete them.
- Places has no developer-operated timeline account to erase. Manage local history in Places, shared files at their destinations, and system backups through iOS. Data you deliberately send in a support request is handled through that contact channel.

## Children

- Places doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

- If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and the new version ships with the app and on the web.
